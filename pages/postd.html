Postd

  • Role: UX/UI designer, Project manager, Copywriter, Github manager and Front-end developer
  • Project Length: January 2026 - April 2026

Postd is a virtual pen-pal web app for Trinity College Dublin students to make new friends and exchange personalized postcards with one another.

We Miss Snail Mail

Bringing Back PenPals

Raise your hand if you’ve pulled up Google and typed in the search bar, “how to make new friends?” We all have and as a university student at an enormous school like Trinity; it’s hard to put yourself out there and make new connections. That’s where Postd comes in!

As a digital penpal program, Postd randomly connects Trinity students with one another to make new friends. Featuring customizable post cards, safety features, public and private messaging; Postd helps students slow down and connect while bringing back a timeless medium.

Cultivating Community

The Community & Connection Pocket Tarot Deck celebrates Washington, D.C.’s diversity; while fostering a sense of pride and belonging amongst it’s residents.

Cultural Representation

Featuring local artists, the BiPoC, and LGBTQ+ communities, this deck enhances the representation and recognition of marginalized communities and how they shaped our nation’s capital.

Educational Resource

The Community & Connection Tarot Guidebook serves as an educational resource highlighting the history of tarot and it’s deep rooted connection to Romani culture. Along with insights and references to Washington, D.C.’s cultural heritage and history.
