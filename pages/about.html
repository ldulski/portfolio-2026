A designer who rolls a D20

Logan St.John is a multi-disciplinary designer, specializing in print, digital, and sound design. With multiple design degrees and a certification in front-end development, they bring the skill of a craftsman to digital design. In all their work, Logan prioritizes community, accessibility, and inclusivity bringing a sense of quality and attention to detail that will make your head spin.

Outside of visual design, they’re conducting design research and actively seeking ways to improve our society using human-centered design. Or they’re out dj-ing, event planning, reading tarot, dancing in the street, etc. I’m currently available for remote design work or in-person work in specific countries.
