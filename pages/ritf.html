Rotten is the Fruit

  • Role: Art Director, Concept Artist, Environmental Artist, Game Designer, and Sound Designer
  • Project Length: May 2026 - August 2026

Rotten is the Fruit is a narrative-driven, adventure game, telling the re-imagined myth of “the Abduction Persephone” set in a futuristic world. This 2.5D game explores what would happen to the world, if Persephone took her fate into her own hands. Told in a dual point-of-view, the player explores the world above and the underworld below as they switch between storylines to uncover the mystery of what happened to the rulers of the Underworld; all while trying to find a way to save their home.
