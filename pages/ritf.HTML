The world is dying. The Gods are restless. For my MSc. in Interactive Digital Media dissertation at Trinity College Dublin, my team and I created the game, Rotten is the Fruit a 2D narrative-rich, platformer game retelling the "Abduction of Persephone" Greek myth.

Play the Demo

Rotten is the Fruit tells the re-imagined Greek myth of the abduction of Persephone. Set in a futuristic world, this 2D side-scroller explores a world where the cycle of life and death has started to collapse. Persephone has disappeared and a mysterious rot spreads above as the line between the world above and the world below unravels. Daphne, an ex-acolyte of Demeter, was exiled from her temple home after a strange interaction with the altar of Persephone resulted in the dawning of this plague upon the lands. Her unexplained immunity to the toxic Rot infecting the world makes her the only person able to learn the truth behind the world’s decay. The player explores the rotting worlds above and the below as they switch between storylines to uncover the mystery of what happened to the rulers of the Underworld; all while trying to find a way to save their home.

As Daphne searches for answers, she discovers Persephone's letters and forgotten artifacts, which allows the player to relive moments from Persephone’s past and ultimately uncover Daphne’s role in this story. Through these intertwined narratives, the mysteries behind the Gods' ancient dealings slowly unfold, revealing that the fate of both worlds rests on Persephone's disappearance and the choices she made to defy her own fate. By taking control over the Rot that’s spread around the worlds of the living and dead, the player learns about the story of Persephone’s Abduction, Daphne’s strange past, and the cosmic ties that link these two women together.

Sound Design

The sound design in Rotten is the Fruit was used to reinforce the differences between the two main environments the Land of the Living and the Underworld. As such, the game’s music and sound effects change based on the player's in-game location. There was no voice acting in this game. Instead, speech samples where recorded, randomized, and stylized for all in-game dialog.
