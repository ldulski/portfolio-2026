Becoming a Joycean Scholar

Behind Retellings

Retellings connects the past and present highlighting the beauty of Dublin through the eyes of the famous author James Joyce and his book “the Dubliners”. Featuring a guided audio walking tour with a Google Maps integration and an in-depth exploration into the life of James Joyce. For the local historian, tourist, or aspiring Joycean scholar, Retellings doesn’t only prioritize Dublin’s history; it prioritizes accessibility and the use experience.
