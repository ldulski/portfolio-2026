Between Frequencies

  • Role: Co-script writer, Voice actor (Voice of Jay), Sound designer, and Co-foley artist
  • Project Length: September 2025 - December 2025

“Between Frequencies” is a supernatural-thriller radio drama with best friends Mia, 16-years-old, and Jay, 17-years-old, going out into the woods alone to break into an abandoned radio station to make contact with another dimension, but unbeknownst to them they make contact with something else lurking in the woods.

You Shouldn’t Be Here

Into the Sound Design

All of the audio for “Between Frequencies” was recorded using a combination of Foley techniques and studio-grade recording equipment. As the sound designer and audio editor, creating the ambiance for this project was similar to putting together a puzzle. Carefully layering each effect while ensuring the vocals were balanced and following the scripts pacing. Listen to an excerpt of the audio below.

Read transcript

Mia: Jay, do you copy?
Jay: muffled garbled speech (Mia – Mia where are you?)
Mia: muttering a mumbled string of curses
Mia: Great…I’m going insane! Sure could’ve used your help like 20 minutes ago! How did you get lost? There’s only ONE path to the radio tower.
Jay: I don’t know! One second I was right behind you, the next I was alone in the middle of the woods! I tried calling you on the walkie but all I got was static until I got closer to the tower.
Mia: Wait...I think someone is following us.
Jay: Who in their right mind would follow us all the way out here?
Mia: I don’t know!?! A deranged psycho killer! An axe murderer! Your parents! Trying to make sure we’re not doing something stupid that will get us both killed!
Jay: Stand back...Ladies first
Mia: Whatever! Dork!

Mia: Come on, we need to find the recording studio or an equipment room to set up in.
Jay: God this place is creepy… no one thought to clean the place up after they shut it down.
Mia: Why would they clean up a shut down radio station? This place was government funded, public-access radio.
Jay: NOPE! No. No. No. No. No. Let’s leave right now.
Mia: Aww…is someone afraid of a little mouse? Come on, it’s gotta be back here.
Mia: Hold up — here we are...Okay what do you think? Can we tap into the radio tower from here? And get a signal out?
Jay: Maybe…I’m pretty sure everything here still works, it's just old and dusty. Give me a minute and let me see what I can do.
Mia: What the?
[Music Box Playing]

Jay: Mia… Mia? Mia!
Jay: You okay?
Mia: Yeah, I’m fine. Can you get it to work?
Jay: Yeah, it’ll be static-y with the storm outside but we should get some signal through. The real trick isn’t getting our message out, it’s getting something back.
Mia: Great lets get started then
Jay: Ready?
Mia: Ready.

Jay: What was that?
Mia: Probably another animal — come on, focus! Are we getting anything back?
Jay: Just static so far. Keep going.
Jay: Mia…the music box…it’s open…
Mia: Ignore it! Keep listening.
Jay: Mia…there’s something out there. In the hallway...
Mia: Lock the door!
Jay: Wait I’m getting something!
Mia: Quick! Write it down!
