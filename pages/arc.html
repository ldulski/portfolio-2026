Airlines Reporting Corporation Customer Satisfaction Survey

  • Role: Graphic designer, Marketing Researcher, and Front-end developer
  • Project Length: June 2023 - August 2023

Identifying new methods to measure customer satisfaction, studying customer experience, and developing a method to collect that data for Airlines Reporting Corporation, ARC.

View Website

Internal Audit

Getting from 1 Star to 5 Stars

An audit of ARC's surveys from past years revealed a lack of consistency in implementing the company's branding, a lack of accessibility, and an unresponsive survey design. After conducting user interviews and meeting with several of ARC's marketing and product design teams, I discovered a desire to create an accessible, easy-to-access survey that could effectively measure customer satisfaction and decrease instances of survey fatigue.

In redesigning the survey, I closely followed ARC's current branding to create a cohesive template for future use that matched the company's visual identity. I designed the survey for multiple screen sizes, followed WCAG guidelines to ensure accessibility, and implemented custom code to create a survey that measures and collects customer satisfaction data using the NPStop3 method that can be easily embedded into email campaigns for efficiency.
